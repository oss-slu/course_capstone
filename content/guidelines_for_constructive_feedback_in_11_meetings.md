## Our "No Backbiting" Rule Explained

During our 1:1 meetings, I want you to feel comfortable sharing honest feedback about your capstone experience, including challenges with teamwork, processes, or leadership. Here's how to do that constructively:

## The Direct Communication Test

**Before sharing critical feedback about a teammate or Tech Lead, ask yourself: "Am I ready to say this directly to them with the goal of continuous improvement?"**

* If yes, great! Let's discuss how to approach that conversation
* If no, let's work together on whether and how to address the issue
* Our 1:1s are a safe space to practice or plan these direct conversations

## Guidelines for Effective Feedback

**Focus on behaviors and impact, not personality**

* ✅ "When sprint commitments change without team discussion, it makes planning difficult"
* ❌ "Sarah is disorganized and doesn't respect the team"

**Be specific with examples**

* ✅ Use concrete situations: "In yesterday's standup when..."
* ❌ Avoid generalizations: "They always..." or "They never..."

**Come with a problem-solving mindset**

* ✅ "Our code review process seems slow. Could we establish clearer timelines?"
* ❌ "Code reviews take forever around here"

**Remember the Prime Directive** *"Everyone did the best job they could, given what they knew at the time, their skills and abilities, the resources available, and the situation at hand."*

Assume positive intent when discussing others.

## What We Want to Avoid

* Complaining about someone's character or personality
* Venting frustrations without intent to improve the situation
* Sharing criticism you're not willing to address directly
* Gossiping about team dynamics

## Remember

The goal is creating solutions and stronger working relationships. Use our 1:1 time to:

* Process challenging situations
* Get guidance on difficult conversations
* Align your capstone work with your career goals
* Share what's working well and what isn't

**Questions? Let's discuss in your upcoming 1:1!**
