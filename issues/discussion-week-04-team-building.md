## Description

**Teams** plan and execute team outings so that relationships deepen beyond transactional project work.

**Instructor** monitors outing planning and checks in on team cohesion so that isolated students are identified.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Slack monitored for outing planning discussions
- [ ] Teams without visible planning followed up
- [ ] Cohesion concerns noted for 1:1s
- [ ] Outing submissions reviewed

## Stretch AC (Team Cohesion)

- [ ] All teams have outing plans or completed outings
- [ ] Outing documentation shows genuine participation
- [ ] Teams showing increased casual communication
