## Description

**Developers** connect with their teams and Tech Leads so that relationships form before code does.

**Instructor** monitors Slack for team introductions and early questions so that disconnected students are identified quickly.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Team channels monitored for activity
- [ ] Students without team connections identified
- [ ] Early questions answered or routed
- [ ] BLA3DE follow-up observed

## Stretch AC (Formation Quality)

- [ ] All Developers have posted in their team channel
- [ ] Teams showing signs of early coordination
- [ ] Tech Leads visibly welcoming new members

## Notes

Week: 1 (Sprint 0)

**Discussion Focus:** Getting to know teammates, understanding project context, establishing communication patterns.

**Observable in:** Slack team channels, DMs (if reported), GitHub introductory activity.

**Activity tie-in:** Team Formation & Onboarding assignment, BLA3DE presentation.
