## Overview

This assignment covers your transition from team formation through the BLA3DE activity to becoming an active contributor on your assigned project team.

## Team Formation: BLA3DE

During the first class session, you will participate in **BLA3DE** - the **B**alanced **L**eadership, **A**llocation and **A**ssignment, **D**emocratic **E**ngagement process. This 90-minute activity uses a "knife fight" metaphor for democratically forming balanced project teams through open negotiation, voluntary participation, and community decision-making.

The process includes:

- **Choose Your Fighter**: Tech Leads deliver 60-second pitches about their projects
- **Initial Deployment**: You physically move to project areas to have conversations with Tech Leads about fit and expectations
- **Tactical Recon & Prisoner Exchange**: Teams balance their composition through voluntary transfers and skill-based exchanges
- **Ceasefire Negotiations & Treaty Signing**: Final adjustments and team ratification

Every individual retains veto power over their placement, and every team collectively decides whether they're ready to move forward.

### Team Announcements

Final team assignments will be announced two days after the BLA3DE activity (Wednesday), following the 48-hour post-meeting survey to confirm satisfaction with placements.

## Developer Onboarding

Once teams are announced, your primary focus shifts to onboarding. This is the core of this assignment.

### Connect with Your Tech Lead

- Introduce yourself to your Tech Lead
- Exchange contact information and confirm communication channels (Slack, Discord, email)
- Schedule or confirm regular meeting times
- Ask questions about the project and team expectations

### Set Up Your Development Environment

- Clone the project repository
- Follow the README and setup documentation
- Install required dependencies and tools
- Verify you can build and run the project locally
- Document any setup issues you encounter for the team

### Begin Team Working Agreement Discussion

- Review the Team Working Agreement checkpoint requirements
- Start discussing team norms, communication preferences, and working hours
- Consider how you'll handle code reviews, meetings, and collaboration
- These discussions will be formalized in the upcoming Team Working Agreement checkpoint

### Prepare for Sprint 1

- Review the project backlog and upcoming sprint goals
- Familiarize yourself with the codebase structure
- Identify areas where you can contribute
- Come to Sprint 1 ready to pick up work

### Participate in Team OKR Drafting

Your team will establish Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) together. As a Developer, your participation is essential:

- Contribute to discussions about what objectives matter to your team
- Help identify realistic key results that reflect the work you'll be doing
- Ensure you understand how your individual contributions connect to team-level goals
- Speak up if proposed OKRs feel unclear or disconnected from the actual work

OKRs are only meaningful when the whole team helps create them.

## Deliverable
