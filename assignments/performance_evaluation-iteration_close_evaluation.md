## Overview

This course uses professional-style performance evaluations to assess your growth and contributions as a software developer. You will receive two comprehensive evaluations during the semester, modeled on real-world performance review processes used in the software industry.

## How It Works

**No submissions required** - Your performance is evaluated based on your ongoing work, participation, and professional conduct throughout the semester. This mirrors how you'll be assessed in your future career.

**Multiple perspectives** - Evaluations incorporate input from:

* Instructor observation and review of work products
* Peer developer feedback and evaluations by Tech Leads
* Client input on collaboration and delivery effectiveness
* Evidence of your collaborative engagement and professional growth

**Written feedback** - You'll receive a detailed written report for each evaluation. You may acknowledge receipt if you choose, and we can discuss any questions you have about the feedback.

## Grade Components (90 points total)

### Discovery (20 points) ###

Demonstrates your engagement with understanding project requirements, exploring solution approaches, and investigating technical challenges. This includes participating in client meetings, conducting research, creating initial designs, and documenting findings. Shows your ability to ask thoughtful questions, gather information effectively, and plan your approach before implementation.

### Delivery (25 points) ###

Measures actual completed work that advances project goals and meets quality standards. This includes merged pull requests, implemented features, resolved issues, and documented solutions. Demonstrates your ability to execute plans, write maintainable code, and produce tangible results that benefit the project and client.

### Collaboration (15 points) ###

Evaluates your effectiveness as a team member through code reviews, retrospective participation, communication quality, and support for teammates. This includes providing constructive feedback, responding to reviews professionally, contributing to team discussions, and helping create a positive working environment.

### Demonstration (15 points) ###

Assesses your ability to showcase and explain your work through demo videos, presentations, and live demonstrations. For sprint-based demonstrations, emphasis is on communication clarity. For final presentations, technical depth is equally important. This prepares you for professional environments where you must regularly present your work.

### Client Satisfaction (15 points) ###

Reflects client feedback on project progress, communication effectiveness, and responsiveness to requirements. Clients evaluate whether the team delivers value, incorporates feedback appropriately, and maintains professional relationships. Demonstrates your ability to work with external stakeholders.

## Getting Feedback

While formal evaluations occur twice per semester, you can request informal feedback at any time if you want to understand how you're tracking in any component area. Professional development is an ongoing conversation, not just a periodic assessment.

## Professional Context

This evaluation approach mirrors real-world software development where performance is measured holistically - not just on code output, but on your effectiveness as a collaborative team member, your communication skills, your ability to understand and solve problems, and your impact on client success. Use this experience to develop the professional habits that will serve you throughout your career.
