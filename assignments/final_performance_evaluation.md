## Overview

This is the final performance evaluation for the semester. It is intentionally scheduled at the end of finals week to allow you to put final touches on your projects before your work is assessed.

This evaluation follows the same professional-style approach as previous evaluations, with one key difference: **this is the only evaluation where your Excellence assignment directly factors into your grade**.

## How It Works

**No submissions required** - Your performance is evaluated based on your ongoing work, participation, and professional conduct throughout the semester. This mirrors how you'll be assessed in your future career.

**Multiple perspectives** - Evaluations incorporate input from:

* Instructor observation and review of work products
* Peer developer feedback and evaluations by Tech Leads
* Client input on collaboration and delivery effectiveness
* Evidence of your collaborative engagement and professional growth

**Written feedback** - You'll receive a detailed written report. You may acknowledge receipt if you choose, and we can discuss any questions you have about the feedback.

## Grade Components (100 points total)

### Discovery (20 points)

Demonstrates your engagement with understanding project requirements, exploring solution approaches, and investigating technical challenges. This includes participating in client meetings, conducting research, creating initial designs, and documenting findings. Shows your ability to ask thoughtful questions, gather information effectively, and plan your approach before implementation.

### Delivery (25 points)

Measures actual completed work that advances project goals and meets quality standards. This includes merged pull requests, implemented features, resolved issues, and documented solutions. Demonstrates your ability to execute plans, write maintainable code, and produce tangible results that benefit the project and client.

### Collaboration (15 points)

Evaluates your effectiveness as a team member through code reviews, retrospective participation, communication quality, and support for teammates. This includes providing constructive feedback, responding to reviews professionally, contributing to team discussions, and helping create a positive working environment.

### Demonstration (15 points)

Assesses your ability to showcase and explain your work through demo videos, presentations, and live demonstrations. For the final presentation, both communication clarity and technical depth are important. This prepares you for professional environments where you must regularly present your work.

### Client Satisfaction (15 points)

Reflects client feedback on project progress, communication effectiveness, and responsiveness to requirements. Clients evaluate whether the team delivers value, incorporates feedback appropriately, and maintains professional relationships. Demonstrates your ability to work with external stakeholders.

### Excellence (10 points)

This component evaluates your completed Excellence assignment. Prior performance evaluations may have discussed progress toward your Excellence goal, but only this final evaluation incorporates the Excellence assignment into your grade. The Excellence assignment accounts for 10% of your overall course grade.

## Grade Expectations

This rubric applies to both this evaluation and the earlier performance evaluations:

### C-Level Performance

Submitting approximately one pull request per sprint demonstrates baseline participation but reflects minimal engagement with the iterative development process. This level of contribution typically results in a C.

### B-Level Performance

To earn a B, you must demonstrate one of the following:

- **Reaching your target Milestones** for each iteration, showing consistent progress toward team goals
- **Documenting adversity clearly** when factors outside your control prevented reaching milestones, including what you learned from the experience and how you adapted

Good-faith effort in the face of adversity is expected. If challenges arise, your response to those challenges matters as much as the outcome.

### A-Level Performance

An A is only possible by completing an Excellence assignment, as it accounts for 10% of the overall course grade. Strong performance across all other components combined with a completed Excellence assignment demonstrates the comprehensive engagement expected for top marks.

## Professional Context

This evaluation approach mirrors real-world software development where performance is measured holistically - not just on code output, but on your effectiveness as a collaborative team member, your communication skills, your ability to understand and solve problems, and your impact on client success. Use this experience to develop the professional habits that will serve you throughout your career.
