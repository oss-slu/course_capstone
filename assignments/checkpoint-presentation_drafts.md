## Overview

Your team will present at the end of the semester (see the [Team Presentation](team_presentation.md) assignment for the full picture). This checkpoint ensures substantive content and a solid demo strategy are in progress before the events.

**This is a HARD deadline.** Submit partial work on time rather than complete work late.

## What You're Preparing

1. **Presentation content** — the substance your audience will engage with
2. **A live demo** — working software ready to show
3. **A pitch skeleton** — a practiced framework you can deliver flexibly

This checkpoint is a **content draft** and a **demo strategy document**.

## Spring Semester (Poster Session)

See [Poster Session Presentation](../content/poster_session_presentation.md) for poster content sections and format required in the Spring semester.

- Draft written content for all applicable poster sections
- Focus on substance, not layout — design comes later

## Fall Semester (Slide Deck)

See [Slide Deck Presentation](../content/slide_deck_presentation.md) for content sections and format required in the Fall semester.

- Draft slide deck outline with content for each section
- Include planned visuals, screenshots, or demo clips
- Speaker assignments for each section

## Demo Strategy (Both Semesters)

Every team must have a live demo ready. Your draft should include a Demo Strategy document covering:

- **What you'll demo:** The specific user flow or feature you'll show, and why it's the most compelling thing to demonstrate
- **Demo walkthrough:** Step-by-step (specific enough to rehearse from)
- **Fallback plan:** What you'll do if the demo breaks (recorded video, screenshots, or something else)
- **Audience adaptation:** How you'll adjust the demo for a technical audience vs. a general one

## Deliverables

- Written content draft (poster sections or slide deck outline, depending on semester)
- Demo Strategy document

## Evaluation Criteria

### Substantive Engagement

Evidence of genuine preparation, including:

- Complete, thoughtful content for all applicable sections
- A realistic and rehearsable Demo Strategy
- Clear identification of your value proposition

## Notes for Developers

- **Everyone pitches:** All team members should be able to give the full pitch — not just introduce themselves
- **Practice the demo:** Know your demo cold; fumbling through it live is avoidable
- **Energy matters:** Practice sustaining your pitch across repetitions
- **Adapt in real time:** Read your audience and adjust how technical you get
- **Career articulation:** Be ready to explain what *you* specifically contributed and what skills it demonstrates

## Notes for Tech Leads

- **One coherent story:** The presentation should have a single narrative, not be a patchwork of individual contributions
- **Connect to strategy:** Your Product Strategy should inform the value proposition
- **Client preparation:** Consider sharing your draft with your client before the showcase
- **Think handoff:** This material may be how future teams first learn about your project
