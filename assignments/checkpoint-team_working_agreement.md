**Evaluation**: Substantive engagement - evidence that the team meaningfully participated in creating the agreement.

**Submission**: Each team member submits a signed copy.

**About the Deadline**: *This is a target deadline. Missing this deadline due to legitimate challenges or circumstances outside your control can demonstrate professionalism through how you communicate and adapt.*
