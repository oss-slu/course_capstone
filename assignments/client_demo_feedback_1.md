## Overview

This is a **bonus opportunity** for Developers who participate in their team's client demo. Your Tech Lead is responsible for preparing and delivering the client demo, but Developer participation is valuable and recognized.

## Purpose

Participating in client demos provides:

- Experience presenting technical work to stakeholders
- Understanding of how your contributions create client value
- Practice communicating with non-technical (or technical) audiences
- Visibility into the client relationship

## Eligibility

You may submit this bonus assignment if you participated in your team's client demo through:

- **Appearing in the demo video**: Presenting a section, answering questions, or actively participating in the live meeting
- **Supporting demo preparation**: Creating demo materials, preparing slides, recording segments, or other documented prep work

## Deliverable

Submit:

1. **Evidence of participation**:
   - If you appeared in the demo: submit the same video your Tech Lead submitted (or link to it)
   - If you supported preparation: submit documentation of your prep work (slides you created, notes, recordings, etc.)

2. **Brief reflection** (required): A sentence or short paragraph documenting your participation - what you contributed, what you learned from the experience

## Evaluation

This is a completion-based bonus. Full credit is awarded for demonstrating meaningful participation in the client demo process.
