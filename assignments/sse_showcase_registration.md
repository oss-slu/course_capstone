## Overview

The SSE Undergraduate Showcase is the School of Science and Engineering's signature annual event highlighting outstanding undergraduate work. Your capstone team will present your project at this event, connecting with the broader SLU community, industry partners, and alumni.

This assignment ensures your team is registered to participate.

## Event Details

- **Event:** 2026 SSE Undergraduate Showcase and SLULaunch Demo Day
- **Date:** Thursday, April 30, 2026
- **Location:** BSC Wool Ballrooms
- **Registration Deadline:** March 1, 2026

## What You Need to Do

### 1. Coordinate with Your Team

Discuss with your team and Tech Lead:

- Who will be present at the Showcase on April 30?
- What aspects of your project will you highlight?
- Who will complete the registration form on behalf of the team?

Only **one team member** needs to complete the presenter registration, but the whole team should be aware of the commitment.

### 2. Complete Presenter Registration

When the registration email arrives (expected January 2026):

- One team member completes the presenter registration form
- Include all team members who will be presenting
- Provide accurate project information

### 3. Confirm Registration

After registering, confirm with your team that registration is complete.

## Deliverable

Submit a brief confirmation including:

- [ ] Your team name and project
- [ ] Confirmation that presenter registration has been completed
- [ ] Names of team members who plan to present on April 30
- [ ] Any questions or concerns about Showcase participation

If your team has not yet received the registration email by mid-February, note that in your submission and the instructor will follow up.

## Evaluation

This is a completion-based checkpoint. Full credit is awarded for confirming your team's registration for the SSE Showcase.

## Why This Matters

The SSE Showcase is an opportunity to:

- **Demonstrate your work** to faculty, staff, industry partners, and prospective students
- **Practice professional presentation** skills in a supportive environment
- **Celebrate your accomplishments** with peers working on similar projects
- **Network with industry partners** and alumni who attend the event
- **Represent your client** and the value of your semester's work

This is one of the most visible moments of your capstone experience. Take it seriously and prepare well.
