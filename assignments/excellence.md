# Excellence Assignment - Capstone Project

## Overview

Excellence recognizes exceptional contributions that go significantly beyond baseline capstone project expectations. Your capstone project represents one semester's iteration of contributions to a larger, ongoing open source product or product ecosystem. This assignment focuses specifically on ways you can extend, enhance, or showcase your contributions to demonstrate professional growth and impact beyond normal project deliverables. Excellence reflects the Jesuit educational value of **magis** - striving for the greater good through exceptional effort and achievement.

**Points**: 10 points total (10% of course grade)
**Minimum Requirement**: Complete one activity that directly relates to and enhances your capstone project contributions
**Nota Bene**: Students may submit an unlimited number of times, and every submission will accumulate towards a total

**Strategic Focus**: Open Source with SLU is working to advance recognition and adoption of the [Rerum](https://rerum.io/) open source product and associated ecosystem as a universal annotation tool. Contributions that drive external adoption, increase community contributions, or integrate Rerum into existing tools are particularly welcome across all categories, especially when they demonstrate measurable impact on the broader ecosystem.

**Note for Rerum OSE contributions**: Teams interested in helping the strategic advancement of the Rerum OSE should consult with the Rerum Playground team and/or the instructor about finding suitable contributions that align with current ecosystem development priorities.

**Collaborative Work**: Collaborative efforts are welcome and encouraged, but each collaborator must make significant individual contributions and be able to articulate and document clearly their individual contributions through objectively verifiable mechanisms (git logs, speaking time, documented responsibilities, etc.).

## Categories & Activities

### Team Leadership (Project-Focused) ###

Demonstrate leadership through your capstone project contributions that advance the strategic direction or governance of your open source product.

**Examples include:**

* Making significant contributions to strategic planning documentation that Tech Leads are responsible for (with clear documentation of individual contributions)
* Leading cross-team coordination efforts for your open source product's development roadmap
* Facilitating major architectural decision-making processes that impact the product's future
* Developing governance frameworks or contribution guidelines for your open source product
* Leading user research or stakeholder engagement initiatives that inform product strategy
* Creating and implementing community-building strategies for your open source product

### Technical Innovation (Project-Driven) ###

Make groundbreaking technical contributions that represent significant advances in your open source product's capabilities or approach.

**Examples include:**

* Developing novel algorithms, architectures, or methodologies that fundamentally advance your product's performance or capabilities
* Creating innovative solutions to complex technical challenges that could be considered industry-leading practices
* Implementing breakthrough features that significantly differentiate your open source product in its market space
* Contributing original research-grade technical work that advances the state of the art in your product's domain
* Developing new technical standards, protocols, or interfaces that could influence broader industry adoption
* Creating innovative integrations or extensions that expand your product's ecosystem impact

### Community Impact (Project-Connected) ###

Build and engage communities around your open source product through sustained outreach and communication efforts.

**Examples include:**

* Building and managing your open source product's social media presence across multiple platforms (with measurable engagement growth)
* Creating, hosting, or regularly contributing to podcasts, vlogs, or video series about your product and its domain
* Securing and delivering guest appearances on established podcasts, vlogs, or industry media discussing your product
* Developing and executing comprehensive marketing strategies that increase product adoption or contributor engagement
* Creating and maintaining developer advocacy programs that build your product's technical community
* Organizing and running community events, meetups, or conferences centered around your product or its ecosystem

**Important**: You must clearly identify which community you are building - product user community, product developer community, or both - and demonstrate sustained impact rather than one-off posts or activities.

### Academic Rigor (Scholarly-Oriented) ###

Pursue scholarly activities that document, analyze, or extend your capstone project contributions through rigorous academic work.

**Point values based on rigor and impact:**

* 2 points: Self-published white paper
* 3-5 points: Technical docs in established venues
* 4 points: Undergraduate poster
* 4-6 points: Case study submission
* 5-7 points: Workshop facilitation
* 6-8 points: Conference presentation, textbook contribution
* 8-10 points: Journal article, **publication-ready book manuscript**

**Note:** Point values are provided for Academic Rigor activities due to established scholarly hierarchies and measurable standards in academic publishing.

**Special Focus**: The Journal of Open Source Software (JOSS) provides an excellent venue for documenting software contributions with academic rigor while maintaining focus on practical impact.

## Submission Requirements

### Documentation ###

Submit a brief report (1-2 pages) that includes:

1. **Activity Description**: What you accomplished and how it connects to your capstone project contributions
2. **Project Connection**: Specific explanation of how this activity directly enhances, extends, or showcases your work on the larger open source product
3. **Evidence**: Links to contributions, presentations, publications, or other verifiable outcomes
4. **Individual Contributions**: For collaborative work, clearly document your specific contributions using objectively verifiable mechanisms
5. **Reflection**: How this activity demonstrates "magis" - going beyond baseline expectations to create greater impact

### Evaluation Criteria ###

* **Direct relevance** to your capstone project contributions to the larger open source product
* **Quality and depth** of the contribution
* **Evidence of impact** beyond your immediate team and normal project deliverables
* **Professional growth** demonstrated through the activity

## Important Notes

* Activities must be **directly related** to your capstone project contributions to the larger open source product - not general professional development
* **Students must go above and beyond normal work**: For example, internal developer services team members would not be able to create reusable frameworks, tools, or resources from their project for other project teams for this assignment, as that is part of their expected role
* You may complete multiple activities and submit each one for accumulated points
* Students can mix and match, making multiple contributions across multiple categories
* Collaborate with your Tech Lead to ensure activities align with project goals and the larger open source product roadmap
* Consider how your excellence activity advances the long-term success and sustainability of your open source product

## Timeline

Excellence activities should be completed and documented by **May 4th** to allow for proper evaluation and recognition of your contributions. Early check-in and active communication about your effort is encouraged.
